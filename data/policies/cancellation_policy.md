# ResolveAI Enterprise Order Cancellation Policy

**Policy Document ID:** POL-CAN-2024-V2
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Department:** Order Management & Fulfillment

## 1. Self-Service and Agent Cancellation Windows
1.1 Orders in **"Processing"** or **"Pending"** status may be cancelled immediately with zero cancellation fees and an instant payment reversal.
1.2 Once an order status transitions to **"In Transit"** or **"Shipped"**, the shipment has physically left the logistics hub and **cannot be cancelled in transit**.
1.3 For orders already in transit, the customer must await package delivery and initiate a standard Return / Refund request under Policy POL-REF-2024-V2.

## 2. Duplicate or Accidental Orders
2.1 If a customer accidentally places duplicate identical orders within a 2-hour window, our automated triage agent flags the secondary order for immediate hold and prompt cancellation assistance.

## 3. Cancellation Financial Reversal
3.1 When a valid cancellation is confirmed before dispatch, hold authorizations on credit/debit cards are released within 24 hours.

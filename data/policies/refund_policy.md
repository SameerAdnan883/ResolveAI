# ResolveAI Enterprise Refund Policy

**Policy Document ID:** POL-REF-2024-V2
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Department:** Customer Experience & Financial Operations

## 1. General Refund Eligibility
1.1 Customers are eligible to request a full refund within **30 calendar days** from the verified delivery date of the item.
1.2 To qualify for a refund, the item must be in its original packaging, unused, and include all original tags, accessories, and documentation.
1.3 Digital goods, gift cards, customized/personalized merchandise, and opened software or hygiene-sensitive items are **strictly non-refundable** unless defective upon arrival.

## 2. Refund Processing and Financial Safeguards
2.1 **Mandatory Human Approval**: Due to financial compliance regulations, any refund exceeding **$150.00 USD** or involving high-risk categories requires explicit authorization from a Human Support Supervisor.
2.2 No automated AI agent is authorized to unilaterally execute direct financial disbursements or credit card chargebacks.
2.3 Approved refunds are returned strictly to the original payment method used at purchase.
2.4 Standard processing time for banking reimbursement is **3 to 5 business days** following refund authorization.

## 3. Shipping Fee Reimbursement
3.1 If a refund is requested due to merchant error, defective merchandise, or confirmed shipping carrier damage, initial and return shipping fees are 100% refunded or credited.
3.2 For discretionary returns (e.g., buyer remorse, ordered wrong color/size without defect), standard return shipping fees of $5.99 may be deducted from the net refund total.

## 4. Late or Missing Delivery Refunds
4.1 If a package has not arrived within **7 business days past the maximum estimated delivery date**, the order is deemed lost in transit, and the customer is entitled to an immediate full refund or complimentary priority replacement.

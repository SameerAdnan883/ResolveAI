# ResolveAI Enterprise Replacement Policy

**Policy Document ID:** POL-REP-2024-V2
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Department:** Logistics & Customer Fulfillment

## 1. Replacement Eligibility
1.1 Customers may request a free replacement for any product that arrives defective, malfunctioning, broken, or mismatched with the ordered specifications.
1.2 Replacement claims must be lodged within **14 calendar days** of verified delivery date.
1.3 If the identical item is out of stock, the customer may choose between:
    - An upgraded model of equivalent value at zero extra cost, or
    - A 100% full refund to original payment method.

## 2. Evidence Requirements for Replacement
2.1 For hardware, electronic devices, or high-value apparel ($75+), the customer must provide a photo or short description showing the defect or damage.
2.2 For items under $30.00 USD, immediate no-return replacement can be authorized to optimize customer convenience and shipping sustainability.

## 3. Shipping and Transit Times for Replacements
3.1 Approved replacements are dispatched via Expedited Delivery (1-2 business days) at no additional shipping charge.
3.2 A pre-paid return shipping label will be generated for the customer to return the original defective item within 21 days if item return is deemed mandatory.

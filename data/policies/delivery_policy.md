# ResolveAI Enterprise Delivery and Shipping Policy

**Policy Document ID:** POL-DEL-2024-V2
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Department:** Supply Chain Logistics & Carrier Operations

## 1. Standard and Express Delivery Timelines
1.1 Standard Shipping: 3 to 5 business days across continental regions.
1.2 Express Priority Shipping: 1 to 2 business days.
1.3 Tracking numbers are activated within 12 hours of order packing.

## 2. Delivery Delays and Carrier Exceptions
2.1 A delivery is categorized as **"Delayed"** when the current date exceeds the stated estimated delivery date by more than 48 hours without carrier status update.
2.2 For minor delays (1-2 days due to adverse weather or carrier sorting bottlenecks), customers receive a carrier status update and a $10 courtesy promotional credit.
2.3 For major delays exceeding **5 business days** beyond the estimated delivery date, the customer may request an expedited investigation. If unresolved after 7 days, the customer is eligible for a full refund or re-shipment.

## 3. Address Modifications
3.1 Shipping address corrections may only be accepted while the order is in "Processing" status.
3.2 Once an item is marked "In Transit", address changes must be arranged directly with the carrier partner (ExpressShip / PostalService) using the official tracking number.

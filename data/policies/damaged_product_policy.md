# ResolveAI Enterprise Damaged & Defective Product Policy

**Policy Document ID:** POL-DAM-2024-V2
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Department:** Quality Assurance & Claims Management

## 1. Damaged Item Reporting
1.1 Any package arriving with visibly damaged packaging or crushed contents must be reported within **7 calendar days** of carrier delivery confirmation.
1.2 The customer service team records the claim under ticket category **"Damaged Item"**.

## 2. Customer Remedies for Damaged Items
2.1 **Immediate Replacement**: The primary remedy is immediate priority dispatch of a brand-new replacement unit at no cost.
2.2 **Full Refund**: If the customer prefers not to wait for a replacement, or if the product is no longer in stock, a 100% full refund is issued.
2.3 **Partial Discount / Keep As-Is**: If the damage is purely cosmetic (e.g. slight scratch on exterior, box dented) and the item functions normally, the customer may elect to receive a **25% partial refund / courtesy credit** to retain the item without returning it.

## 3. Return of Damaged Goods
3.1 For hazardous or severely broken glass/liquid items, the customer is instructed NOT to return the item for physical safety. Photographic documentation suffices.
3.2 High-value electronics ($150+) require return with pre-paid postage so engineering QA can inspect manufacturing or transit defects.
